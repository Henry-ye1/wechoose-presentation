Design opportunity Give friends a reason to compare choices and talk while they browse.

Persona & opportunity

Meet Lin Yue.

Observed patterns

Friends sometimes browse without a set list. They stop to point out a snack that fits a friend’s taste or brings up a shared joke.

Before buying, they consider whether they want the item and would use it.

Design hypothesis

A short shared challenge could make the supermarket a place friends choose to meet up.

Design question

How might we help friends play together while they shop?

Value proposition

A supermarket game for two friends.

  • Try a different kind of outing.
  • Find out what your friend would pick.
  • Save on eligible items you choose to buy.

Service overview

From phone to shelf to checkout.

  • Phones

    Show each player’s prompts and track the discount.

  • Shelves

    Give friends real products to compare, find and scan.

  • Checkout

    Apply savings to the eligible products they keep.

Storyboard

Out for movie snacks.

  1. Start together

    Lin and her friend pair their phones and choose the snack aisle.

  2. Guess her pick

    Her friend guesses popcorn. They reveal the match together.

  3. Solve it together

    They combine “sweet” and “made from grains” to find the shortbread.

  4. Roll together

    They choose to stake part of their offer and roll one die each. A total of 7 or more triples their stake.

  5. Check out

    They pay the final price for their snacks and the ¥2 fee for the pair.
